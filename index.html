The playbook

Three plays. The rules are written by Ticket Operation.

Every fan gets a clear rule for acquisition, engagement and defense, for STMs, single-ticket buyers and merch fans, and the brain keeps it current every day.

Attack

Conversion score
Who is ready to buy next?
  • Single-game buyers moving to a half-season or full plan
  • Merch buyers who have never bought a ticket
  • Fan Genome lookalikes of the best STMs
  • Journey: Ticket Ataque · Agents: SDR, Ticket & Merch Sales

Midfield

Upgrade & engagement
Who should come more, and spend more?
  • Plan usage alerts when seats go unscanned
  • Upgrade candidates and guests who could become holders
  • Miami Miles to reward every visit and purchase
  • Journey: Meio de Campo · Agent: Concierge

Defense

Churn & renew
Who is about to leave?
  • STMs at risk: low attendance, late payments, no engagement
  • Renewal window: automatic, assisted and special cases
  • Win-back of members who did not renew
  • Journey: Renewal · Agents: Renew, Churn Specialist

A director builds the rule. No ticket to IT.

Goal40 renewal meetings
AudienceDefense ≥ 0.7, window open
ChannelsWhatsApp, email, rep
Touch cap3 a week
ApprovalCRM lead
Scores from Cogny Intelligence (CFI indices) in Data Cloud; the highest of the three decides the play
